# Privacy Policy — Onsen Planner

**Effective date:** 12 June 2026
**Last updated:** 12 June 2026

Onsen Planner ("the App") is built and published by Nils Johansson ("we", "us"). This policy describes what data the App handles and what it does not. It is written to be read, not skimmed.

## Short version

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party tracking, and no servers operated by us. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

All content you create or import in the App — week notes, memos, expenses, events, contacts, photos, currency preferences, region settings, and other configuration — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData and `UserDefaults` APIs. It is not uploaded to any server we control.

If your device backs up to iCloud through iOS's standard system backup, your App data may be included in that backup. That backup is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not this one.

## System permissions the App may ask for

When you use specific features, iOS will ask you to grant the App permission to access certain system data. You can decline any of these and the rest of the App continues to work.

- **Contacts** (`NSContactsUsageDescription`) — Used only when you choose to import a contact into the App's people list. Imported information is stored locally on your device. The App does not read your contacts in the background and does not transmit them anywhere.
- **Photo Library** (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`) — Used only when you choose a photo to attach as a contact's profile picture. The selected image is stored locally with the contact and is not transmitted anywhere.
- **Calendar** (`NSCalendarsFullAccessUsageDescription`) — Used only to display your existing calendar events inline with the weekly view. The App reads events from your local/synced calendars to show them to you on your device. It does not create, modify, or transmit your events.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security**.

## What the App does NOT do

- No analytics or telemetry (no Firebase, no Sentry, no Crashlytics, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no first-party logging back to us).
- No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
- No third-party tracking SDKs.
- No account creation, login, or user profile system.
- No network requests to servers we operate. The App ships with no backend.
- No selling, sharing, or licensing of personal data to third parties — there is no data to sell.

## Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data export and deletion

You can delete all App data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device. Because nothing is stored on a remote server, uninstalling removes your data; we cannot recover it after that point because we never held a copy.

If you exported a PDF, CSV, or shareable card from the App, that exported file lives wherever you saved or sent it (Files, Mail, Messages, etc.) and is outside the scope of this policy.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the **Effective date** above will be updated and the new version will be published at the same URL. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
